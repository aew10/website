Stars in Hobart

This meeting continues the Stars in… conference series, with topics including:

  • Asteroseismology, Stellar activity, and Solar physics
  • Clusters and stellar populations
  • Stellar evolution and Supernovae

AEW12 – Australian Exoplanet Workshop

AEW12 will continue the Australian Exoplanet Workshop series, covering observational surveys, atmospheric characterisation, and host star properties.

Conference Schedule:
Mon 23 – Tue 24 Nov: Stars in Hobart
Wed 25 Nov: Shared day on Star–Planet Interactions
Thu 26 – Fri 27 Nov: AEW12 – Exoplanet Workshop
